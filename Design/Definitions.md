## Action

> A game "action" that can be taken by a [bot](#bot) within a match (e.g. "Move Left", "Jump", "Attack", etc).
<!-- taken by an avatar? -->
---

## Avatar

> Playable character(s) in physical game space.
<!-- controlled by a bot -->
---

## Bot

> A non-human player of a game as defined by a submission. Encompasses all submissions from a given team.

---

## Client

> An interface which encapsulates a submission and provides libraries for the purpose of connecting to the engine in order to play the game.

---

## Competition

> The event hosted by a third party using the BitBattle [system](#system).

---

## Component

> A component is a grouping of related functionality encapsulated behind a well-defined interface. Components are *not* separately deployable units. For further reading see [C4 Abstractions](https://c4model.com/abstractions/)

---

## Container

> Not Docker. A container represents an application or a data store. A container is something that needs to be running in order for the overall software system to work. For further reading see [C4 Abstractions](https://c4model.com/abstractions/)

---

## Engine

> The engine is the library on which the [game](#game) is built. This is designed with a clear contract so that it can be rewritten by ambitious [competition developers](./Requirements/Actors.md#competition-developer).

---

## Feed

> A shared visual representation of [matches](#match) which any [spectator](./Requirements/Actors.md#spectator) can tune into. The canon "what" is happening right now.

---

## Game

> The "game" which the competition designers built for use by bots. If you were given a normal set of controls, you could play it as you would any normal video game.

---

## Leaderboard

> A visual representation of [tournament](#tournament) standings.

---

## Match

> An individual run of a game between [submissions](#submission).

---

### Replay

> A viewable object generated by the [simulation](#simulation) of a match.

---

### Simulation

> The act of [bots](#bot) running in the game environment to determine match results and generate the match replay (or: actively running the code).

---

## Player

> Prefer [Bot](#bot) instead.

---

## Round

> One or more [matches](#match) between [bots](#bot) to be calculated for [tournament](#tournament) scoring.

---

## Rule

> A standard instruction that defines what a [competitor](./Requirements/Actors.md#competitor) or [bot](#bot) can or cannot do in a [competition](#competition) as defined by the [competition hosts](./Requirements/Actors.md#competition-host).

---

## Run

> Prefer [Match](#match) instead.

---

## Submission

> An individual instance of the logic of a bot (the code) written by a competitor, as uploaded and submitted to the tournament.

### Active Submission

> A submission which is actively running in tournament cycles and available to test against other active submissions.

### Current Submission

> The most recent submission of a team, which if not replaced, will be the final submission for the competition.

### Test Submission

> An individual instance of the logic of a bot (the code) as written by a competitor, as uploaded to the tournament, viewed, and deleted.

---

## System

> The [engine](#engine), runtime environment, database setup, and interacting [containers](#container) that interact to form a shippable product.

---

## Team

> An entity corresponding to an individual or group of [competitors](./Requirements/Actors.md#competitor) that is used by the [system](#system) to track authorship of [submissions](#submission).

---

## Test

>A non-[tournament](#tournament) [match](#match) between submissions for the sake of validating bot logic in the [game](#game) environment. Tests are private and can output debug logs.
---

## Tick

> The singular turnover of the [game](#game) running over the state of the [game](#game) and processed actions and triggers.

---

## Tournament

> A set of matches complete with each competitor's (active? current?) submission, ran against as many other (active? current?) submissions as tournament structure demands.

---

## Turn

> A tick at which a bot is given the chance to take an action.
