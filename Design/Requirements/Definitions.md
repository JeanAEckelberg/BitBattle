## Action

> A [game](#game) "action" that can be taken by a [bot](#bot) within a [match](#match) (e.g. "Move Left", "Jump", "Attack", etc).

---

## Avatar

> Playable character in physical [game](#game) space controlled by a [bot](#bot).

---

## Bot

> A non-human [player](#player) of a [game](#game) as defined by a [submission](#submission). Encompasses all [submissions](#submission) from a given [team](#team).

---

## Category

> Buckets teams are assigned to (ie: collegiate and alumni) which can be used for the purpose of awarding prizes (or more).

---

## Client

> An interface which encapsulates a [submission](#submission) and provides libraries for the purpose of connecting to the [engine](#engine) in order to play the [game](#game).

---

## Competition

> The event hosted by a third party using the BitBattle [system](#system).

---

## Component

> A [component](#component) is a grouping of related functionality encapsulated behind a well-defined interface. [Components](#component) are *not* separately deployable units. For further reading see [C4 Abstractions](https://c4model.com/abstractions/)

---

## Container

> Not Docker. A [container](#container) represents an application or a data store. A [container](#container) is something that needs to be running in order for the overall software system to work. For further reading see [C4 Abstractions](https://c4model.com/abstractions/)

---

## Engine

> The [engine](#engine) is the library on which the [game](#game) is built. This is designed with a clear contract so that it can be rewritten by ambitious [competition developers](./Requirements/Actors.md#competition-developer).

---

## Feed

> A shared visual representation of [matches](#match) which any [spectator](./Requirements/Actors.md#spectator) can tune into. The canon "what" is happening right now.

---

## Game

> The challenge in which [bots](#bot) contend according to a set of [rules](#rule). It is built by the [competition developers](Requirements/Actors.md#competition-developer). If you were given a normal set of controls, you could play it as you would any normal video [game](#game).

---

## Leaderboard

> A visual representation of [tournament](#tournament) standings.

---

## Match

> An individual [run](#run) of a [game](#game) involving one or more [submissions](#submission).

---

## Player

> Prefer [Bot](#bot) instead.

---

## Replay

> A viewable object generated by the [simulation](#simulation) of a [match](#match).

---

## Round

> One or more [matches](#match) to be calculated for [tournament](#tournament) scoring.

---

## Rule

> A standard instruction that defines what a [competitor](./Requirements/Actors.md#competitor) or [bot](#bot) can or cannot do in a [competition](#competition), as defined by the [competition hosts](./Requirements/Actors.md#competition-host).

---

## Run

> Prefer [Match](#match) instead.

---

## Simulation

> The act of one or more [bots](#bot) running in the [game](#game) environment to determine [match](#match) results and generate the [match](#match) [replay](#replay) (or: actively running the code).

---

## Submission

> An individual instance of the logic of a [bot](#bot) written by a competitor, as uploaded and submitted to the [tournament](#tournament).

### Highest-Scoring Submission

> A [submission](#submission) which is actively running in [tournament](#tournament) cycles, is compared to the current submission.

### Current Submission

> The most recent [submission](#submission) of a [team](#team), which, unless specified otherwise, will be the final [submission](#submission) for the [competition](#competition).

### Test Submission

> An individual instance of the logic of a [bot](#bot) as written by a [competitor](./Requirements/Actors.md#competitor), as uploaded to server components (NOT THE TOURNAMENT), viewed, and deleted once testing is completed (NO DATA RETENTION).

---

## System

> The [engine](#engine), runtime environment, database setup, and interacting [containers](#container) that interact to form a shippable product.

---

## Team

> An entity corresponding to an individual or group of [competitors](Requirements/Actors.md#competitor) that is used by the [system](#system) to track authorship of [submissions](#submission).

---

## Test

>A non-[tournament](#tournament) [match](#match) for the sake of validating [bot](#bot) logic in the [game](#game) environment. [Tests](#test) are private and can output debug logs.

---

## Tick

> A single iteration of the [game](#game) loop.

---

## Tournament

> A set of [matches](#match) complete with each [team's](#team) (active? current?) [submission](#submission).

---

## Turn

> A set of one or more [ticks](#tick) at which a [bot](#bot) is given the chance to take an [action](#action).

---
