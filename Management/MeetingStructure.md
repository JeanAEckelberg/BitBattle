# Meeting Structure

- 15 min casual get together
- 5 min recap (meeting notes, TODOs, etc.)
- 10-15 min standup
- 5 min agenda discussion
- 30 min group work
- Remaining time as needed
- 10 min retrospective

## Meeting Roles

- Jean: Chair/Facilitator
- Ada: Notetaker
- Chris: Backup Chair/Facilitator, Timekeeper
- Seris: Designated backup catch-all

## Discussion Procedure

1. Discussion topic will be presented
2. Each Member will present their argument
3. A vote will be called to determine if the discussion topic requires more time to be devoted to it.
   1. If the discussion topic requires more time, the Meeting Leader will decide on the time allotted for the discussion.
      - At any time, a call for a vote may be made.
      - If seconded, the person who called the vote may present the options for voting or defer it to the Meeting Leader. Go to step 5.
   2. 5-minutes before the end time, each Member will present their final argument or position.
4. The Meeting Leader will present the options for voting.
5. Each Member will cast their vote. Abstain votes will go in favor of the most time-efficient option.
